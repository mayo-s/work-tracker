Privacy policy

As of: 11.11.2026

A translation for your information; only the German version is binding.

1. Controller

2. Overview

Work Tracker is a web app in which you record your working hours and activities. It processes only the data needed to use it. There is no tracking, no analytics, no advertising, and the pages load nothing from third-party services (no external fonts, scripts or CDNs). Your data is not sold and is passed on only to the processor named below. There is no automated decision-making and no profiling.

3. Hosting

The app runs at Amazon Web Services (Amazon Web Services EMEA SARL, 38 Avenue John F. Kennedy, L-1855 Luxembourg) as processor (Art. 28 GDPR). The data is stored in Frankfurt am Main (region eu-central-1).

Requests pass through Amazon CloudFront, whose servers are in Europe and North America; AWS processes your IP address as technically necessary to deliver the page. A transfer to the USA is possible: Amazon Web Services, Inc. is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (adequacy decision, Art. 45 GDPR). The app itself stores no IP addresses; CloudFront access logs are switched off. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in secure and reliable operation (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Warnings and errors of the app are logged in Amazon CloudWatch, without the contents of requests such as form data or cookies, and deleted after 30 days. To recover from errors, the database keeps restore points of the last 35 days; deleted data is therefore still contained there for up to 35 days.

4. Cookies

The app sets only three cookies, all strictly necessary for what you request: the login and the language you choose (Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG); no consent is needed for them. The app stores no other data in your browser.

  • wt_session: keeps you logged in; expires 24 hours after your last activity (for the operator account "root" after one hour) or when you log out.
  • wt_browser: only if you choose to remember this browser at two-factor login; spares you the code for 30 days.
  • wt_language: only if you choose a language at the top; shows the pages in that language, also before the login; expires after one year.

5. User account

For your account we store your username, e-mail address, first and last name, your password (only as a non-reversible hash), with two-factor login its key and the hashes of the recovery codes, your role, your team and your team role, whether you handle support, and the time of registration and of the last login. The purpose is the login and the use of the app, the legal basis the user relationship (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). The data stays stored as long as the account exists, also when it is deactivated.

You delete your account yourself in the settings ("Delete account", with your password), or an admin deletes it at your request. With the account, all your data is deleted: the time tracking (section 6), your settings, your team memberships, also former ones, the logs about your account and about who viewed your reports; your messages through the contact form are anonymized at once (section 7). Where you appear in other people's data, for example as a team admin who viewed a report, as the sender of an invitation or with a change in the log, the record stays without your name ("Deleted user"). The log keeps only that an account was deleted, by whom and when, not which one. Full backups (section 8) and the database's restore points (section 3) still contain the data until they are replaced or deleted after 35 days.

Failed logins and registrations are only counted with their time, without IP address and without account, to slow down the guessing of passwords, and deleted after one minute (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

6. Time tracking and teams

We store what you record: working days with times and breaks, entries with duration, activity, ticket, service and description including their change history, tickets, services, activity types, work schedules, PTO, notes and settings. The purpose is the time tracking itself (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). The data stays stored as long as your account exists. With "Export my data" in the settings you download it as a file at any time.

If you are a member of a team, its team admins and the auditors appointed for the team see your reports (dashboard, week, month, year), read-only, for the time of your membership; not your day notes and the review list. Every such look is logged; you see the log in your settings, as well as who can look. The purpose is organizing the team's work (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Changes to roles, teams and the status of accounts, and to your own login (password, two-factor login), are logged with who, what, whom and when, so that they can be traced (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). This log and the log of looks at reports stay stored as long as the account exists.

7. Contact form

When you write to us through the contact form, we store your name, e-mail address, subject and message, for logged-in users also the account, to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR); the answer comes by e-mail. Only users with the support role read the messages and their internal notes. 90 days after a request was marked as done, the name, e-mail address and the link to the account are removed; subject, message and notes are kept without any reference to you, so that earlier problems can be found. On request we delete a message earlier.

8. Full backup

The operator can download a full backup of all data, encrypted with a passphrase, to restore the app after a failure (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The operator keeps it encrypted and deletes it once a newer one replaces it.

9. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20; also through "Export my data") and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21). For this, write to the e-mail address given above or use the contact form. You delete your account with all its data yourself in the settings, or we delete it on request (section 5).

You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), in particular in the member state of your residence, your place of work or the place of the alleged infringement.

The account details are needed to use the app; without a name and an e-mail address we cannot answer a message. Beyond that, you are not obliged to provide any data.
